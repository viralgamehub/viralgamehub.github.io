Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers the Viral Gaming Hub app and its games ("the App"), developed by Viral, Tucumán, Argentina.

What we collect

  • Account data (optional): email or sign-in provider ID and a display name, used to log in, save progress, show leaderboards and connect with friends.
  • Game data: scores, progress, achievements, friend lists and online room activity.
  • Purchases: handled by Google Play Billing. We receive purchase confirmations, never your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Service providers

Account and game data are stored with Supabase (database and authentication). Packs are downloaded from GitHub. Purchases go through Google Play.

Your choices

You can play without an account. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing viralgamingservice@gmail.com.

Delete your account: in the app (Profile → Delete account) or without the app: viralgamehub.github.io/delete.html. Deletion removes all your account data.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We'll update this page if the policy changes.

Contact

viralgamingservice@gmail.com
